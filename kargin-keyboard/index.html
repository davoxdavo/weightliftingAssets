Կարգին հայերեն ստեղնաշար

Kargin is an Armenian keyboard for iPhone that punctuates for you, guesses your next word and learns the way you write.

Features

  • Automatic punctuation: commas, ՞ and ՝ where Armenian grammar wants them
  • Predictions that learn from you, with the right word forms
  • Adjustable height: shorter or taller keys with a slider
  • Dictation in Armenian without internet, right on your iPhone
  • Անքֆուր: curses swapped for funny alternatives everyone still understands
  • Armenian letters, phonetic typing with Latin letters (barev → բարև) and English
  • Templates, emoji suggestions, Kargin sticker packs and clipboard history

Private by design

Your vocabulary, word combinations and templates stay on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
