Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn the keyboard on?

Open Kargin and follow the Setup screen, or go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose Kargin. Then hold the globe key in any app to switch to it.

Do I need to allow Full Access?

No. Typing, predictions and automatic punctuation work without it. Full Access adds clipboard history and paste, stickers, templates, sounds and haptics, and lets the keyboard share what it learns with the app. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I type Armenian with Latin letters?

Tap the alphabet key to switch to phonetic mode and type "barev" to get the Armenian word. The spelling rules can be changed in the app's settings.

The commas or question marks are in the wrong place

Press Backspace right after the keyboard adds a mark to take it back. Automatic punctuation can be turned off in the app or from the keyboard's settings.

How do I make the keyboard taller or shorter?

Use the Keyboard Height slider in the app, or open the keyboard's settings from the alphabet key menu.

Does dictation need the internet?

No. Speech is turned into text on your device. A transcription server is used only if you enter your own in Settings.

What is ANQFUR?

The curse filter. It replaces a curse word with a funny alternative everyone still understands, or only suggests one. You can switch it off, or choose suggest-only, in the app.

How do I delete what the keyboard learned?

In the app, open Your Words: swipe a word to hide it from suggestions, or reset everything the keyboard learned.
