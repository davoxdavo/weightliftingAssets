7. Health and fitness disclaimer

Gym Logbook is a logging tool, not a medical device and not medical, training, or nutritional advice. Estimated one-rep max and similar calculations are approximations only. Consult a qualified professional before starting or changing an exercise program. You use the App at your own risk.

The energy estimate is an estimate, not a measurement. Where you enable it, the App calculates an approximate energy cost for a finished session on your device. It does so by estimating your resting metabolic rate from the height, weight, year of birth, and sex you enter, using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation (1990), and multiplying it by a published activity value for the kind of training each exercise represents, taken from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities, over the recorded duration of the session. Neither the load you lifted nor any effort mark affects the result.

If you enable the optional heart-rate chart, the App also reads heart-rate readings recorded by Apple Health for the minutes of a finished session, and uses their average to estimate the energy cost of the cardio part of that session with a published equation (Keytel and colleagues, 2005). It does not use heart rate to estimate the cost of resistance training: heart rate rises during heavy or static effort for reasons that do not track energy expenditure, so applying that equation to lifting would report energy you did not spend. The chart itself is a display of readings recorded by your device or watch. It is not a medical measurement, is not a diagnostic tool, and must not be used to detect, monitor or make decisions about any heart condition. If you have concerns about your heart rate, consult a physician.

Published measurements of the energy cost of resistance training vary widely between individuals and between studies, so the figure is deliberately rounded and shown as an approximation. It is not a medical or nutritional measurement, must not be used to make medical decisions or to plan clinical nutrition, and should not be relied on where accuracy matters. Where the App writes this estimate to your health store, it is written as an estimated active energy value; if another device or app also records the same session, your totals may count it more than once. Consult a qualified professional — for example a physician or registered dietitian — before relying on energy figures to change how you eat or train.

The exercise illustrations in the App are generated by AI and may depict a movement inaccurately. They are provided only to help you recognise an exercise at a glance; the written instructions and safety notes are the reference, and you should read them. Do not rely on an illustration as a demonstration of correct or safe technique — if an image looks wrong, or you are unsure how to perform a movement, ask a qualified coach first.
