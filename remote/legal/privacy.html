Privacy Policy

Gym Logbook · Last updated: 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gym Logbook (“App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App. By using the App, you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Overview

Gym Logbook is designed to be local-first: workout logs primarily live on your device. Optional features—sign-in, cloud sync, health export, friends and sharing, community exercise statistics, analytics, and crash reporting—process additional data as described below. Two things are worth stating up front: the App never reads your health data except heart rate, and only if you turn that on (section 2.3), and the height, weight, year of birth, and sex used by the optional energy estimate stay on your device unless you sign in, in which case they are also stored with your account so that your devices agree (section 2.2) — and nowhere else.

2. Information we process

2.1 On your device

Depending on how you use the App, we may store locally:

  • Workout sessions, sets, notes, templates, custom exercises, supersets, and personal records
  • App preferences (language, rest-timer defaults, Health export toggle, and similar settings)
  • Cached catalogs, translations, and form-guide images downloaded from our content CDN
  • If you turn on the optional energy estimate: the height, weight, year of birth, and sex you enter for it. While you are signed out — including as a guest — these four values stay only on this device. If you are signed in, they are also saved to your account so your other devices show the same figures instead of asking you to enter them again; see section 2.2. They are never included in analytics. They are also included if you choose to create a backup file yourself (section 2.8), because a backup that dropped them would silently turn the feature off when you restored it.

2.2 Account and cloud sync (optional)

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we process account identifiers and profile metadata needed to authenticate you (such as a user ID, and display name or email when the provider shares them). When cloud sync is enabled for a signed-in account, workout and library data associated with that account may be stored on our cloud backend so you can restore or use it across devices.

Device session information (for example device model, OS version, app version, locale, and last-active time) may be stored to show your signed-in devices and support remote sign-out.

Body values for the energy estimate. If you have turned the optional energy estimate on, the four values it needs — height, weight, year of birth, and sex — are stored with your signed-in account as well, so that a second device you sign in on shows the same figures instead of asking you to enter them again. They are stored as all four together or not at all, and never for a signed-out or guest session. They are never used for advertising or profiling, never included in analytics, and not shared with other users or with any third party. Deleting your account deletes them.

2.3 Health export — Apple Health / Health Connect (optional)

If you enable Health export, the App may write finished workouts to Apple Health (or Health Connect on Android) as workout samples, plus distance when you authorize it.

Heart rate is the only thing the App reads, and only if you turn it on. Health export is otherwise write-only. If you enable the optional heart-rate chart, the App reads heart-rate readings from Apple Health for the minutes of a workout you finished, copies them onto that workout so the chart still works later, and uses the average to estimate the energy cost of the cardio part of that session. It reads no other kind of health data at any time, it reads nothing for periods you were not training, and it does not monitor your heart rate in the background. With the setting off, the App requests no read access at all and imports nothing.

Heart-rate readings copied this way are stored on your device with the workout, are included if you create a backup file yourself (section 2.8), are never used for advertising or profiling, are never included in analytics, and are not shared with other users or with any third party. Turning the setting off stops all further reading; readings already copied stay with their workouts until you delete those workouts.

Energy is written only if you ask for it twice. If — and only if — you have enabled Health export and turned on the optional energy estimate and granted the energy permission, the App also writes an estimated active energy value for each finished workout. If any of those is off, no energy value is written at all. The figure is an estimate calculated on your device from the height, weight, year of birth, and sex you entered — it is not a measurement, and it is not taken from any sensor. If you also wear a watch that records the same session, both may write energy and your daily totals can count it twice; the App tells you this when you turn the estimate on.

Turning the estimate off stops future writes. It does not remove energy values already written to your health store — you can delete those in the Health app, which is the only place they live.

Apple’s (or Google’s) privacy terms apply to data stored in your health store.

2.4 Community exercise statistics

So the App can show which exercises and supersets are commonly trained, a signed-in device adds to shared counters when you finish a workout. Two kinds of counter exist:

  • Your own totals, stored on your account so your devices agree about which exercises you use most. This is visible only to you.
  • Shared totals, one record per exercise (and per built-in superset) in our catalog, counting how many workouts and sets it has appeared in across all users, together with aggregate amounts such as total reps or seconds. These records contain no user identifier, no name, no date, and no individual workout. They are counters only, and they cannot be traced back to a person from their contents.

Exercises you created yourself are never included in the shared totals — only exercises from our built-in catalog are counted, so a custom exercise name never leaves your device this way. The shared totals record only that a catalog exercise was trained and how much; they do not record when, by whom, or in what workout.

2.5 Friends, sharing, and shared sessions (optional)

If you use the friends features, additional information is processed so other people can see what you choose to share with them:

  • A basic profile (display name, nickname, and photo if you set one) that other signed-in users can see when you invite them or they invite you.
  • Invitations you send or accept, including the link or code and who redeemed it.
  • Friend connections and the permissions attached to them. You choose, per friend, what they may see — for example workout history, personal records, planned sessions, streaks, or whether you are currently training. Nothing is shared by default; each permission is off until you turn it on, and you can withdraw any of them or remove the connection at any time.
  • Items you deliberately share, such as a workout or template sent to a friend.
  • Shared training sessions. If you train together with a friend, the session's exercise plan and each participant's presence and last-seen time are processed while the session is live so both of you see the same plan. You each log your own sets.

Information you share with a friend is visible to that friend. We cannot un-share something another person has already seen, and removing a friend stops future access rather than erasing what they saw.

2.6 Game Center (optional)

If you take part in the streak leaderboards, your score is submitted to Apple Game Center under your Game Center identity, which is managed by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy terms. You control Game Center in iOS Settings, and the App works fully without it.

2.7 Analytics and crash reporting

We may collect anonymized or pseudonymous product analytics (feature usage events) and crash diagnostics to improve reliability. These services may include device and app technical information (such as OS version, app version, and crash stacks). We do not sell your personal information.

2.8 Export / import

If you export or import a backup, the file you choose to share or restore is under your control. We do not receive exported files unless you send them to us yourself.

3. How we use information

  • Provide logging, history, templates, sync, and related App features
  • Authenticate accounts and manage device sessions
  • Operate maintenance, force-update, catalog, and translation updates
  • Show which exercises are commonly trained, using aggregate counters (section 2.4)
  • Let you connect with friends and share only what you choose to share (section 2.5)
  • Calculate the optional energy estimate on your device, and keep the values it needs consistent across the devices you are signed in on (sections 2.1 and 2.2)
  • Draw a heart-rate chart for a finished workout, and cost its cardio part from your measured rate, if you turn that on (section 2.3)
  • Diagnose crashes and understand product usage at an aggregate level
  • Comply with law and protect the security of the service

4. Legal bases (where required)

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the App you request); your consent (for example optional Health access or analytics where consent is required); and legitimate interests (security, reliability, and product improvement) that do not override your rights.

5. Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the App:

  • Service providers such as Apple, Google, and Firebase for auth, hosting, analytics, and crash reporting
  • Other users you choose to share with — only the specific information you grant a friend access to, and only for as long as you leave that permission on (section 2.5)
  • Legal requirements when we believe disclosure is required by law or to protect rights and safety

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it (including via in-app delete / Recycle Bin flows) or remove the App. Cloud account and synced data are retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for backups, abuse prevention, or legal obligations. You may request account deletion where the App provides that control; local workouts may remain on device after account deletion unless you remove them separately.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a consumer fitness logbook (including transport encryption for network requests and provider-managed authentication). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

9. International transfers

If you use cloud features, your data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate transfer mechanisms provided by those vendors.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Use the App as a guest without linking Apple or Google
  • Disable Health export in Insights settings
  • Turn the energy estimate off in Account → Preferences, which stops any further energy from being written to your health store; delete energy already written using the Health app
  • Withdraw any friend permission, or remove a friend connection, at any time
  • Delete energy, workout, or distance samples the App wrote using your health store's own controls
  • Sign out, revoke other devices, or delete your account where offered
  • Export or delete local data using in-app tools
  • Control system permissions (Health, notifications, and similar) in iOS Settings

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at davit.ghushchyan@gmail.com or via Support to exercise those rights.
